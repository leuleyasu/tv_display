## 1. Restaurants & Food Courts

### FREE Core Utility Offered (Phase 1):
1. **Real-Time Digital Menu**: Instant price updates (<500ms) without paper menu printing.
2. **Universal Payment QR Code**: Cleans off 5–10 messy bank stickers from dining tables; accepts Telebirr, CBE Birr, Awash, Dashen via LakiPay.
3. **Live DSTV Football Match Mode**: Displays Premier League & Champions League in 75% screen space while keeping venue food/drink specials running on the side.
4. **Out-of-Stock & Chef's Special Badges**: Managed in 2 seconds from the manager's phone.

### Target Advertisers (Phase 2):
- **Beverage Brands**: Bottled water, soft drinks, beer brands, juices.
- **Food Delivery & Tech Apps**: Deliver Addis, BeU, Ride, Feres.
- **Banking & Financial Services**: Awash Bank, Telebirr, Dashen Bank, CBE.

---

## 2. Cafes & Coffee Shops

### FREE Core Utility Offered (Phase 1):
1. **Morning Breakfast & Pastry Slides**: Automatic time-based menu transitions (Morning Coffee → Lunch Specials).
2. **Universal Table QR Payment**: Fast payment for takeaway coffee customers.
3. **Free Wi-Fi QR Code**: Guests scan the TV to connect directly to the venue's Wi-Fi.
4. **Live Time & Ticker Widget**: Displays current time, morning news headlines, and weather.

### Target Advertisers (Phase 2):
- **Ride Hailing Apps**: Ride, Feres, ZayRide (High intent during morning commute).
- **Telecom & Digital Wallets**: Telebirr, Ethio Telecom.
- **Co-working & Business Services**: Software apps, laptops, courses, banks.

---

## 3. Gyms & Fitness Centers

### FREE Core Utility Offered (Phase 1):
1. **Workout Class Schedules**: Displays daily trainer schedules (Zumba, Crossfit, Aerobics, Yoga).
2. **High-Tempo Fitness Visuals**: Motivational quotes, energy level indicators, and high-energy ambient visual loops.
3. **Hydration & Gym Announcements**: Reminders to stay hydrated, gym rules, and membership renewal specials.
4. **Workout Music Requests**: Members scan QR codes to request workout tracks.

### Target Advertisers (Phase 2):
- **Fitness & Supplement Brands**: Protein powders, pre-workouts, vitamins.
- **Healthy Meal Prep & Nutrition**: Healthy food delivery, salad bars, juice bars.
- **Activewear & Sportswear**: Athletic shoes, gym apparel, fitness trackers.
- **Energy Drinks & Water**: Electrolyte drinks, bottled mineral water.

---

## 4. Lounges & Bars

### FREE Core Utility Offered (Phase 1):
1. **Live Sports Match Center HUD**: Live football scores, goal alerts, match minute counters.
2. **Guest Song Requests & DJ Shoutouts**: Customers scan QR code to request songs or send VIP birthday shoutouts.
3. **Cocktail & Premium Drink Menu**: Dynamic visual display of premium bottle menus and lounge specials.

### Target Advertisers (Phase 2):
- **Nightlife & Beverage Brands**: Premium spirits, beer brands, energy drinks.
- **Sports Betting Apps**: Live match odds and betting promotions.
- **Event Organizers**: Concert tickets, festival announcements.
