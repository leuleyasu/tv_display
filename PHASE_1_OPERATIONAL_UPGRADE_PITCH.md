# Phase 1: Operational Upgrade Pitch Script (The Trojan Horse)

## Strategy Overview
In Phase 1, sales representatives pitch venue owners (restaurants, cafes, lounges) **strictly on operational utility**. We DO NOT mention ad revenue or sponsor ads at this stage to eliminate skepticism or suspicion.

**The Goal**: Get the venue owner to agree to a 100% FREE installation of the HoursSignage TV display system on 1 or more TV screens within 2 minutes.

---

## The Phase 1 Pitch Script

### 1. The 30-Second Hook
> **"Good afternoon [Manager/Owner Name]! We are upgrading restaurants and cafes in Addis Ababa with a FREE Smart TV Display System. We replace your expensive printed paper menus with a real-time Digital Menu on your TV, clean off all those 10 messy bank QR stickers from your dining tables, and give your guests live DSTV football match stats—100% FREE for your venue. Can we set it up on 1 TV to test for 7 days?"**

---

## 2. The 4 Operational Value Pillars

### Pillar 1: FREE Real-Time Digital Menu
- **Sales Rep:** *"Whenever ingredient or item prices change, you don't need to re-print paper menus or cross out prices with a pen. You update prices or mark items 'Out of Stock' in 2 seconds from your phone, reflecting instantly on the TV screen and customer phone menus."*

### Pillar 2: Eliminates Table QR Clutter (Unified LakiPay)
- **Sales Rep:** *"Right now, your dining tables have 6 to 10 messy bank QR stickers (CBE Birr, Telebirr, Awash, Dashen, Abyssinia) cluttering table space. We put ONE Universal QR Code on the TV screen. When customers scan it, they see your digital menu and can pay using ANY bank or wallet seamlessly via LakiPay."*

### Pillar 3: Live DSTV Football Match Center HUD
- **Sales Rep:** *"When Premier League or Champions League matches play, our app doesn't close! It automatically switches to Match Mode, displaying live sports in 75% to 85% of the screen while keeping your food/drink specials active on the side banner."*

### Pillar 4: Zero Cost & Zero Maintenance
- **Sales Rep:** *"There is zero installation cost, zero hardware cost, and zero monthly subscription fee. We handle software updates automatically."*

---

## 3. Handling Phase 1 Questions

| Venue Question | Sales Response |
| :--- | :--- |
| **"Why is this software free for my restaurant?"** | *"We provide the digital menu & TV software platform to build network partnership with top Addis venues. There is no catch and no subscription fee."* |
| **"How fast do menu price updates take effect?"** | *"In less than half a second! You change a price on your phone or laptop dashboard, and it updates on the TV screen and customer phones instantly without rebooting."* |
| **"Do I need to buy a new TV?"** | *"No! Works on any existing Smart TV, Android TV, or TV stick already in your venue."* |

---

## 4. Closing Phase 1

> **Sales Rep:** *"Since there is zero financial risk and 0 ETB cost, let's install it on your main dining area TV right now so you can test the free digital menu and table QR cleanup. Shall we set it up?"*
