# 🗺️ Master Go-To-Market Plan: Phased Cluster Strategy

## Executive Summary

**Do NOT try to sign 100 venues randomly all over the city before talking to advertisers.**  
Likewise, **do NOT pitch big corporate advertisers with 0 active screens.**

The winning playbook is a **3-Phase High-Density Cluster Strategy**:  
Start by securing **15–20 flagship venues in ONE high-traffic commercial area (e.g., Bole / Kazanchis)**, launch with 2–3 **Founding Brand Sponsors**, and use that revenue to scale to **100+ venues city-wide**.

## 📍 Phase 1: High-Density Seed Cluster (15–20 Venues)

### Why 15–20 Venues First?
- **Speed**: You can onboard 15–20 top venues in **10–14 days**.
- **Geographic Proximity**: All venues are close to each other, making installation, troubleshooting, and photography fast.
- **Immediate Critical Mass**: 20 screens × 300 visitors/day × 30 days = **180,000 monthly attentive impressions**. That is a real, high-value media package for advertisers!

### Ideal Target Venue Profile:
- **High Foot Traffic & Dwell Time (45+ mins)**: Popular cafes, lounge bars, casual dining restaurants, sports lounges.
- **Visible TV Monitors**: Venues that already have 43"+ TVs mounted in main dining areas.

## 💼 Phase 2: Anchor Advertiser Pitch

Once your 15–20 venue cluster is live, pitch **2 to 3 Founding Advertisers**:

### The Pitch Offer: "Bole Premium Media Network"
- **Impressions**: 180,000 attentive monthly views across 20 top venues.
- **Price**: **60,000 ETB / month per sponsor** (Discounted launch rate).
