# Phase 2: Ad Network Activation & Venue Revenue Sharing

## Strategy Overview
Phase 2 occurs after the venue has successfully onboarded with Phase 1 (Operational Upgrade) and is actively enjoying their free Digital TV Menu and clean tables.

**The Goal**: Activate the DOOH ad rotation network, explain the 3-Shift Dayparting revenue model, present competitor exclusion guarantees, and deliver the monthly **9,000 ETB passive revenue payout check**!

---

## 1. The Phase 2 Reveal Script (Day 14 / Month 1 Payout)

> **"Good afternoon [Manager/Owner Name]! We hope you've enjoyed your free Digital TV Menu and clean tables this month. As part of our HoursSignage partner venue network, top corporate sponsors (Telebirr, banks, beverage brands) ran background ad rotations during display loops. Today, we are excited to deliver your venue's first monthly revenue payout check of 9,000 ETB!"**

---

## 2. Explaining the Revenue Engine to the Venue Owner

### 1. How the Revenue is Generated
- Non-intrusive 15-second sponsor ads play during idle screen loops, sidebars during DSTV football matches, and ad overlays.
- Ads are scheduled according to our 3-Shift Dayparting system (**Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner peak hours**).

### 2. Strict Competitor Exclusion Guarantee
- **Sales Rep:** *"We guarantee 100% competitor protection! We will NEVER show ads for competing restaurants or cafes on your TV screens. We only display non-competing national brands like Telcos, Banks, Car Apps, and non-alcoholic beverages."*

### 3. Transparent Monthly Payout Structure
- **Gross Revenue Generated / TV Screen**: 12,000 ETB / month
- **Venue Revenue Share (75%)**: **9,000 ETB / month** *(Paid directly via Telebirr or Bank Transfer on the 1st of every month)*
- **HoursSignage Platform Share (25%)**: 3,000 ETB / month *(Covers tech, servers, and ad sales operations)*

---

## 3. Scaling to Multiple TVs

Once the venue owner receives their first **9,000 ETB payout check**, upsell them to install `night_track_tv` on all remaining TVs in their venue:

> **Sales Rep:** *"You earned 9,000 ETB this month from 1 TV screen! If we connect your other 2 TVs in the VIP section and upstairs lounge, your monthly passive revenue will increase to 27,000 ETB every month."*
