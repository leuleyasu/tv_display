## 1. The Stealth "Trojan Horse" Pitch Strategy (RECOMMENDED)

### Why Hide the Ad Network Initially?

When venue owners hear _"We want to put ads on your TV and pay you free money"_, some get skeptical or suspicious.

Instead, use the **Trojan Horse Strategy**: Pitch the TV system as a **FREE Restaurant Operational Upgrade** that solves their daily headaches (paper menus, table clutter, DSTV management).

```
   PHASE 1: THE OPERATIONAL HOOK (Day 1)            PHASE 2: THE REVENUE BONUS (Day 14)
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐       ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Pitch as a FREE Restaurant Upgrade:     │       │ Activate Ad Rotation & Send Monthly Cash│
│ • Free Digital QR Menu                  │ ────► │ • "By the way, sponsor ads ran this   │
│ • Clean up 10 Messy Table Bank QRs      │       │   month! Here is your 9,000 ETB revenue │
│ • DSTV Football Match Center HUD        │       │   payout check!"                        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘       └─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### The Stealth Pitch Script:

> **"Good afternoon [Manager/Owner Name]! We are upgrading restaurants and cafes in Addis Ababa with a FREE Smart TV Display System. We replace your expensive printed paper menus with a dynamic Digital Menu on your TV, remove all 10 messy bank QR stickers from your tables using 1 Universal Payment QR Code, and give your guests live DSTV football match stats—100% FREE for your venue. Can we test it on 1 TV for 7 days?"**

Once installed and running, the venue owner loves the digital menu and clean tables. On Day 14/30, you activate the sponsor ad loop and hand them their first **9,000 ETB payout check** as a pleasant surprise bonus!

## 2. Full Venue Pitch Script (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Identify Pain Points (Build Rapport)

- **Sales Rep:** "I noticed you have 3 TVs in the dining area. Right now, they're either showing satellite TV news or turned off. Plus, your dining tables have 6 different bank QR stickers (CBE Birr, Telebirr, Awash, Dashen) which clutter the tables."
- **Sales Rep:** "Also, whenever ingredient costs change, how much do you spend re-printing paper menus or crossing out prices with a pen?"

### Step 2: Introduce the Solution (HoursSignage)

- **Sales Rep:** "We provide **HoursSignage**, a smart TV signage system built specifically for Ethiopian venues."
- **Sales Rep:** "We install our software directly onto your TVs for **0 ETB**. It gives you 4 massive benefits:"

1. **Passive Monthly Income (9,000 ETB / Month)**:
   - "We connect your TV to our city-wide advertising network (Telebirr, banks, beverage brands). We pay you **9,000 ETB cash every single month** just for leaving your TV on."
2. **FREE Real-Time Digital Menu**:
   - "No more re-printing paper menus! You can update item prices or mark items 'Out of Stock' in 2 seconds from your phone. It updates instantly on the TV and customer phones."
3. **One Universal LakiPay Payment QR Code**:
   - "Remove all those messy stickers from your tables! We put **One Universal QR Code** on the TV screen. When customers scan it, they see your menu and can pay using **Telebirr, CBE Birr, Awash, Dashen, or Bank of Abyssinia** via LakiPay."
4. **Live DSTV Football Match Mode**:
   - "When Premier League or Champions League matches play, our app doesn't close! It switches to **Match Mode** (L-Bar split screen or floating dock), showing live sports in 75% of the screen while keeping sponsor ads and your venue menu active!"
